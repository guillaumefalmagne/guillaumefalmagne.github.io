Teaching

Philosophy: A course rarely makes students master its content; it should rather give them the vocabulary, intuitions and anchors to dig deeper when they meet a concept again "in real life". In teaching, I put the intuition before the technique and explain the motivation behind every technicality; in mentoring, I balance autonomy with support.
